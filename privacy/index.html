Privacy

This informational website may generate standard hosting and security logs. It does not intentionally use behavioral advertising, fingerprinting, invasive tracking, third-party font requests, or nonessential cookies.

01 — This website

No forms. No account system.

Cinqic.com is a static GitHub Pages site. No forms or account systems are currently present, and the site does not send product content to a service from the browser.

02 — Product privacy

Each product has its own boundary.

The website does not replace product-specific documentation.

Juniper

Juniper has no telemetry, analytics, advertising SDK, crash reporting, or account. A configured remote provider receives the request sent to it, and Juniper labels that route REMOTE. Desktop provider credentials use the operating-system credential store and Android provider credentials use Android Keystore. Its repository documents route labels, private chats, attachments, tools, and platform limitations in full.

Cinqic Calculator

Calculator’s Windows, Linux, and Android applications work fully offline without an account, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or transmission of calculations, history, or settings. The Android app requests no permissions.
