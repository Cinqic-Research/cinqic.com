Cinqic Calculator

A private, offline-first calculator for Windows, Linux, and Android. See the whole expression and its answer as you type, convert common units, review local calculation history, and use practical financial tools — without an account, cloud service, or internet connection.

Download for Windows ↓ Download for Linux ↓ Download for Android ↓ View source

Windows 10/11 · Linux x86_64 · Android 5.0+  |  v1.1.0

01 — Overview

Useful without an account, and without AI.

Cinqic Calculator is a small, standalone tool for Windows, Linux, and Android. It works entirely offline and does not use or need AI.

01

Live answers

The whole expression stays on screen and its result appears as you type, before you press equals.

02

Standard & scientific

Arithmetic, percentages, parentheses, powers, roots, trigonometry, logarithms, factorials, and more.

03

Unit conversion

Length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, and data storage — decimal and binary units kept distinct.

04

Financial tools

Discounts, sales tax, tips, bill splitting, and simple & compound interest estimates.

05

Local history

Recent calculations stay on your device, with an easy way to disable or clear them.

03 — Installation

Getting started

Windows

  1. Download the installer above and run it.
  2. Follow the setup prompts (no administrator privileges needed).
  3. Launch Cinqic Calculator from the Start menu.
  4. Use it fully offline — no account or internet connection required.

Android

  1. Download the APK above on your device.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If prompted, allow “Install unknown apps” for the app you used to open it — a normal Android requirement for any app installed outside the Play Store, not something specific to Cinqic Calculator.
  3. Launch Cinqic Calculator from your app drawer.
  4. Use it fully offline — no account or internet connection required, and the app never asks for one.

04 — Privacy

Works fully offline

Cinqic Calculator does not require an account, does not include advertising or analytics, and never transmits your calculations, history, or settings anywhere — on Windows, Linux, or Android. Settings and optional local history stay on your device: in your Windows user profile, under ~/.local/share on Linux, or in the Android app’s private storage (which no other app can read, and which is removed automatically if you uninstall). The Android app requests no permissions, including no internet permission. See the app’s privacy documentation for details.

Ecosystem

A standalone product, independent of Juniper.

Cinqic Calculator works entirely without artificial intelligence, on every platform. Juniper is a separate Cinqic local-first AI app; there is currently no Juniper integration with Cinqic Calculator being advertised. AI should remain a choice.
